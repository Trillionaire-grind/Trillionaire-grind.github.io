Questions

Do I need a gym?

The program is built for a gym. Tell me what you have access to on your application and I will adjust.

Do I have to give up foods I like?

No. Your meal plan is built around what you already eat. You learn portions, not a new diet.

What if I need help with lifting technique?

Start light and learn each lift before adding weight. For hands-on help, I will connect you with my trainer partner. Those sessions are separate and paid directly to them.

Is there in-person training?

This program is remote. If you want a trainer and meals handled for you, ask about the done-for-you program on your call.

How do I pay?

Pick a plan on the form and Stripe checkout opens after you apply. Weekly bills every week. 3, 6, and 12 month plans are paid up front and do not auto-renew. Leave the plan blank if you want to choose on the call.

Can I cancel?

Every plan has a 3-month minimum. After that, weekly plans continue week to week and you can cancel with 7 days notice. Prepaid plans run for the period you paid for and do not auto-renew.
