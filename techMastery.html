Why directors put this on the calendar

It answers the question families keep asking you.

Scam calls, scam texts, and fake pop-up warnings target your residents every week, and families worry about it out loud. Residents leave this hour with four specific warning signs they can use the same afternoon.

It is a hard slot to fill well.

Technology sessions usually fail because the presenter moves at their own speed and the room quietly gives up. Nothing here is rushed, and no resident is made to feel behind.

Families hear about it.

A resident who can start a video call without help calls home more often. That shows up in your family feedback and in your tours.

What your residents learn

  1. Why they cannot break it

    The belief that stops most people from ever trying, and the simple routine that fixes the majority of problems before they call their children.

  2. Their phone, set up properly

    Larger text, louder ringer, silencing unknown callers, and sending a photo to a grandchild without it failing.

  3. How to spot a scam in ten seconds

    The four warning signs behind every scam call, text, and pop-up, including the grandchild emergency call and the fake refund.

  4. How to join a video call

    Zoom, FaceTime, and the doctor's telehealth link all work the same way once you have seen it once.

  5. What artificial intelligence actually is

    A plain explanation, what it is useful for, what it gets wrong, and the one AI scam every resident and family should know about.

About the presenter

Siguineau is a Naples-based speaker and author who teaches technology to people who were never taught it. He has taught this material to residents at Seascape here in town, and he is an active member of Naples Sunrise Bay Toastmasters, where he holds a club leadership role.

He speaks the way he was taught to speak: slowly, with one idea at a time, and with no assumption about what the room already knows.
